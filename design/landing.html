Straight answers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Message AnyStudio on WhatsApp and it works there. If you prefer a bigger screen, the web app has the same library, your credit history and your brand kits waiting.

Will it change what my product looks like?

The product itself is preserved — we relight it and change what is around it. If a generation does alter the product in a way you did not ask for, that is a bug, and the credit goes back to your balance.

What happens when my credits run out?

Nothing disappears. Your library, brand kits and connected accounts stay exactly as they are — you just cannot generate anything new until you top up or your plan renews.

Why is the price different for me?

Prices are set per market rather than converted from dollars at the day's rate, so what you see is what you pay every month — it does not move when the exchange rate does.

Can my whole team use one account?

On Business, brand kits are shared across your workspace. On Organization you get seats with roles, separate projects, and an audit log of who generated what.
