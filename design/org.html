Back Step 4 of 4

Bring your team in.

You can do this later, but the organization is more useful with more than one person in it — and the audit log only means something when it has names.

What the roles can do
OwnerBilling, contracts and deleting the organization. Exactly one, and it can be handed over.
AdminProjects, API keys, members and brand kits. Everything except billing and deletion.
MemberGenerates work in the projects they are added to. Cannot see the others.
BillingInvoices, credit history and usage. Cannot generate anything.
AuditorReads usage and the audit log. Changes nothing. For finance and security reviews.
What happens when you want to go live. Live keys need business verification — a registration number and the billing entity. Invoicing and net terms need a contract. Security review, DPA and sub-processors are answered on our trust page without a call, if that is faster for you.
